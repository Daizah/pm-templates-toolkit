## Agenda

### 1. Welcome & Introductions (5 min)
- Facilitator opens the meeting
- Each attendee introduces themselves and their role on the project

### 2. Project Overview (10 min)
- Review the project charter summary
- Business need and why this project matters
- High-level goals and expected outcomes

### 3. Scope Review (10 min)
- Walk through what is in scope
- Clarify what is out of scope
- Open floor for questions

### 4. Timeline & Milestones (10 min)
- Review key milestones and target dates
- Identify any known constraints or hard deadlines
- Confirm phase ownership

### 5. Roles & Responsibilities (10 min)
- Confirm each team member's role and key responsibilities
- Clarify decision-making authority
- Identify escalation paths

### 6. Communication Plan (5 min)
- Meeting cadence (weekly standups, status check-ins, etc.)
- Primary communication channels (Slack, email, Jira, etc.)
- Status reporting schedule and format

### 7. Risks & Open Issues (5 min)
- Highlight top 2-3 risks identified so far
- Open floor for team to surface additional concerns
- Note items for the RAID log

### 8. Q&A & Next Steps (5 min)
- Open floor for questions
- Confirm next meeting date and owner
- Assign any immediate action items
